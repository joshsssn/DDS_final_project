\medskip
\textbf{Raw data.} French Wikipedia dump of \textbf{1 October 2026} (\texttt{frwiki-20261001}): tables
\texttt{page}, \texttt{pagelinks}, \texttt{linktarget}, \texttt{redirect}, \texttt{page\_props}
(2.76\,GB compressed), each file SHA-1 checked against the official checksum file.
Our streaming parser keeps \emph{every} row: its counts (14,148,116 pages; 470,370,087 links;
31,672,724 link targets; 2,071,832 redirects; 8,480,889 page properties) equal an independent
\texttt{zcat | grep -c} count.
The game needs a graph whose nodes are \emph{articles} (namespace 0, not a redirect) and whose edges
are clicks a player can make. We measured first, then removed only what is not such a node or edge.

Self-loops and duplicates (after redirect resolution) &
Of the 238,762,721 candidate edges: 53,339 self-loops (0.022\%), 2,806,457 duplicates (1.18\%), e.g.\ a page linking both to X and to a redirect of X. &

\normalsize
\medskip
\textbf{Result.} 470,370,087 raw link rows $\rightarrow$ 238,762,721 candidate edges $\rightarrow$
\textbf{235,902,925 edges} between \textbf{2,782,511 articles} (mean out-degree 84.8, median 40).

\textbf{What we deliberately did not change.} No sampling, imputation or artificial noise; titles kept
exactly as in the dump. Links added by templates (navigation boxes, authority control) are \emph{kept}:
\texttt{pagelinks} records them, they appear on the page and a player can click them. They distort
out-degree, so instead of removing them we measure the distortion separately (bias check on 200
pages through the MediaWiki API).
